橘子老師插圖樣張

Four candidate styles for the 37 example-word pictures (roadmap item 4-1). Each row draws the same four words next to the current cat, so you can see which style sits best with her. 包子, 杯子 and 燈 are words where the emoji is wrong or unclear today.

The cat in rows A, C and D is the exact catSVG('happy') from index.html. Row B redraws her without outlines, because that style would also need a new mascot. The small row under each style is the size of a choice button on a phone (44 px).
